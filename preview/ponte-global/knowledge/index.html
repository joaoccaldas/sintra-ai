What’s here, how it’s checked, and where to begin

38 cited briefs · 23 downloadable replication skills · 256 existing prompts. Skills and full briefs are two formats of the same knowledge.

  • Prompt library: starting prompts and sample outputs.
  • Automation playbooks: recurring patterns to adapt.
  • Guides, learning paths and concepts: foundations and deeper explanations.
  • Tools, models and news: reference and discovery; check individual provenance and dates.

Source-checked means an editor read the cited primary material. Customer stories remain attributed reports. Practice cases describe expected results; only an explicit execution receipt establishes a completed test. Older reference collections may lack review metadata.

Daily builds refresh discovery feeds and validate the release. They do not automatically verify an announcement, test a new provider workflow or renew a source-check date.
