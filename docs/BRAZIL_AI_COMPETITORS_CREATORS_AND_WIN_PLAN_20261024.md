# Sintra Brasil — AI communities, creators, media and education competitive study
**As of:** 2026-10-24 · **Market:** Brazil · **Evidence:** public price pages, official product websites, app listings, screenshots, research and creator-audience estimates · **Type:** strategic analysis, not audited operating results.
**Existing engineering context:** integrated beta PR #31, local exercise PR #32, model pilot PR #33. This file belongs to the existing strategy draft PR #15 to avoid another planning PR.

## 0. Executive decision

**Do not market Sintra primarily as the largest Brazilian AI-news aggregator.** IA Brasil (ianobrasil.com.br) reports 229 sources, regional coverage, investments, regulatory indicators and about 99 Brazilian use-case entries on 24 October 2026. Sintra's Brazilian radar had only two seed publisher feeds in beta. Alura, DIO, ibe.IA, Mestres IA, Academia Lendária and I2A2 already use combinations of guided learning, practical projects, communities, mentoring and creator channels.

**Recommended wedge:** A Portuguese-first, trustworthy mobile product that lets people from different professions turn one news story or real work problem into a **completed, reviewed AI-related outcome**, then helps them explain it, share it and improve it with a community. No account required to try. Member permissions and model inference gated separately.

Positioning proposal: **"ond.ai Brasil — IA que você entende, testa e aplica."** Keep "Sintra Brasil" as a temporary codename until naming clearance: a distinct established company sells "Sintra AI" agents at sintra.ai. Do not claim legal conflict conclusively, but treat brand/domain clearance as P0 before paid acquisition.

## 5. SWOT of Sintra as actually built

**Strengths:** ond.ai's editorial visual differentiation; source-linked news architecture and provenance tests; first working notes-to-actions lab, manually reviewed outputs; lower first-load JS on homepage; branch-gated news publication fixes; existing large content catalog, two seed Brazilian RSS publishers, 11 international feed adapters; HoldingCo RLS foundation and disabled authenticated model pilot.

**Weaknesses:** zero validated organic audience, qualified leads, paid customers, churn cohorts or ambassador network; public consumer Auth and newsletter still absent; community/forum not live; only one in-browser deterministic task; publisher-derived Brazil relevance, not original country analysis; many legacy deep pages lack unified UX; PT/EN toggle not full indexable locale routing; temporary beta URL and brand/domain naming conflict.

**Opportunities:** Brazilian everyday professional roles, SMB owners and education partnerships; original source → practical outcome cards; domain-specific shareable results and member projects; public skills/claims validation and editorial transparency; city/regional partner cohorts, bilingual international research adapted by Brazilian editors.

**Threats:** IA Brasil's stronger news source index; already well-established local media/course creators; low trust of AI-generated summaries; YouTube/Instagram distribution gatekeeping and referral economics; paid-model provider and privacy cost; copyable content; existing separate commercial brand "Sintra AI" at https://sintra.ai/ (brand clearance P0).

**Conclusion:** do not try to be "all in one AI everything"; build the most trustworthy and easy **applied outcomes graph** by profession.

## 6. Product focus, moat and mobile model

### Initial segment
Prioritize **Brazilian micro/small service businesses and nontechnical workers** (sales, admin, marketing, finance, customer service), with free student/educator access. Validate across at least two geographic regions and varying digital skill levels. Why: existing note extraction, practical prompt inventory, founder domain expertise, business willingness to pay via training. Secondary: builders/educators as ambassadors, not initial mass targeting.

### Product architecture and three promises
1. **Descobrir:** Brazil-first, 1–3 source-attributed important signals, explicit published/retrieved/reviewed timestamps, genre/relevance tags. World follows. Avoid claiming domestic event solely because publisher is Brazilian.
2. **Testar:** 3- to 7-minute PT-BR mini-labs that actually operate on user input; result + original source/why it works + human review. Local/private when possible; model-backed only with opt-in/backend quotas.
3. **Compartilhar:** optional account to save reviewed projects, annotate source/evidence, ask for constructive improvements. Moderation before public publishing. Rewards for human-reviewed outcomes, not view farming.

**Recommended mobile primary bar after community launch:** Hoje | Explorar | Praticar | Comunidade | Perfil. Until community is real, retain one "Mais" navigation; no dead tab. Secondary UI: search, source filters and collections behind contextual affordances. No multilevel hamburger maze as default.

### Distinct durable advantage
A typed, portable **Action Receipt** links: topical event/source → short pedagogic explanation → runnable method/version → input privacy level → outcome + observed constraints → reviewer/human feedback → adapted follow-up. Searchable by profession and region. Pure content is easily copied; a corpus of grounded, versioned, truly tried outcomes plus reviewers is harder to reproduce.

## 7. Content and creator distribution engine

- **Video top-of-funnel:** shorts/reels/tiktok showing a *specific problem → 3-second demonstration → visible evidence → call-to-action to the matching free lab*. 3–5 short prototypes weekly during pilot if a human editor can actually verify them. Don't auto-publish inaccurate generated demos.
- **YouTube intent:** one 5–10 minute authentic walkthrough per week; overlay original sources, screenshots, and failure cases; captions, timestamps and useful download/reusable action receipt.
- **WhatsApp:** user-triggered share and clearly opted-in community links; don't auto-add people or send unsolicited blasts.
- **Newsletter:** start with one editorial weekly digest when confirmed email consent + provider delivery/bounces/unsubscribe exist. 3 news, 1 verified task, 1 community project and 1 correction/failure note. Daily only if supported by evidence and retention.
- **LinkedIn / SME distribution:** one professional workflow teardown or original, source-linked Brazil case per week, with light B2B CTA. Partner with trusted local educators/SME associations rather than only large paid influencers.
- **Search/GEO:** original structured PT-BR outcome pages, accurate dates, evidence, linked primary source, visible author/editor/review version, actual `/pt-br/` and `/en/` pages with hreflang; no low-quality mass-generated SEO content.
- **Creator partnerships:** 5–10 micro/niche creators in administration, design, Bahia/local commerce, finance and maker education. Test qualified completed-lab users per collab, not followers or raw views; transparent affiliate/referral disclosure.

### Distribution experiment matrix (targets are proposals, not actual baselines)

| Experiment | Primary signal | Continue rule |
|---|---|---|
| 10 short videos around 3 labs | View → clicked lab → **completed meaningful task** | At least 2 genuinely qualified experiments outperform generic AI tip posts; measure by cohort |
| WhatsApp share after completed action | Share → returning verified action | Positive shares and repeat trial, with no spam/complaints |
| Weekly human-reviewed digest | Confirmed subscribers → clicked lab → action completed | Consistent engagement, very low unsubscribes and corrections |
| 3 niche creators / SME educators | Activated users + cost per useful result | Sustainable quality and creators willing to repeat |
| One Brazil business cohort | 5–10 owners finish same workflow and report usefulness | Time saved and human correction rate collected, not only testimonials |

## 8. Revenue and sales model (hypotheses to validate)

**Do not monetize access to basic knowledge, original news or basic local workflows.** Charge for scarce *help, reliability, collaboration, deployment and measured business outcomes*.

| Segment | Hypothesized offer | Proposed price to test, not validated willingness-to-pay |
|---|---|---|
| Public | Trustworthy news, first 3+ labs, beginner paths, read approved projects | Free |
| Pro learner | Save multiple projects, guided feedback, more authorized model runs, workshops/demos, personal learning receipts | A/B test R$29–49/month or annual pack, AFTER activation evidence |
| Practitioner | Advanced workflows, templates, curated peer workshops, priority reviews | Interview/test R$79–129/month |
| Team training | Employee onboarding, progress evidence, private projects, role-based guides, source/review logs | Pilot-specific R$500–2,000/month plus setup/usage, NOT a published rate card |
| Consultancy/cohort | One measurable problem, diagnosis, workflow, evaluation and human upskilling | Pilot quotes R$4,000–12,000 depending on scope; outcomes and margin verified |
| Sponsors/affiliates | Clearly labeled sponsors of digest/videos and ethical tools | Only after genuine audience and editorial independence; no paid news rankings |

### Transparent scenario arithmetic (illustrative gross revenue, not forecast)
- 250 paying Pro members × R$39/month = **R$9,750/month gross**, before churn, tax, model cost and acquisition.
- 1,000 paying Pro × R$39/month = **R$39,000/month gross**.
- 5 one-off B2B training pilots × R$6,000 = **R$30,000 one-off gross**, not MRR.
- If model usage, moderation, payment costs and CAC exceed gross contribution per retained user, the consumer tier is not a viable standalone business. Track LTV/CAC by cohort and gross margin, not only ARPU.

**Preferred near-term commercial path:** sell two evidence-backed SME/team upskilling pilots before spending heavily on paid consumer acquisition. Partner sales can fund responsible free education without shutting beginners out.

## 9. Execution: staged 90-day plan and go/no-go

### Days 0–14: evidence and first five outcomes
- Resolve Sintra AI brand collision via legal/domain clearance; decide whether public name should instead be "ond.ai Brasil / ond.ai Prática".
- Perform 20 Brazilian user interviews (minimum 10 nontechnical employees, 5 SME owners, 5 student/educator/creators), deliberately recruiting beyond one city/income stratum.
- Observe 5 real beginner task attempts at 360–430px, record completion time and confusion at every tap. Verify value without login.
- Stabilize PR #31/#32; preview and QA beyond homepage; add two additional local/demo labs (e.g. convert invoice lines to spreadsheet, WhatsApp customer response) with real sample tasks and privacy.
- Choose 3–5 topic clusters only (SMB sales, finance/admin, creator workflows, student, public services); do not launch empty categories.
- Create canonical action receipts and link first Brazilian story to a runnable lab only when evidence supports it.

### Days 15–30: controlled Brazil pilot
- Private beta 50–100 opt-in users across at least two regions, verified through user recruitment not follower metrics.
- Opt-in sign-in/member profile when HoldingCo Auth redirect+RLS and moderation checks pass; keep anonymous local labs.
- First weekly manually reviewed newsletter after double opt-in/bounces/unsubscribe tested; no automatic send from `konamundo@gmail.com` without configured provider/permission.
- 10 authentic vertical short demos, 2 YouTube long demos, 3 creator collaborations; each directs to a specific no-signup lab.
- Collect funnel: landing unique visitors → lab starts → source-reviewed completion → voluntary share → 7-day meaningful return. Privacy-minimal events and consent.
- **Gate:** ≥30 reviewed useful results and ≥50% first-lab success for recruited task-intent testers (proposed thresholds, adjust based on observed difficulty). Fail -> improve usability and task value, not buy traffic.

### Days 31–60: functioning trusted community
- Launch one moderated project format (problem, input privacy, reproducible method, output, review status and learning). Avoid a general unmoderated forum.
- Recruit 10 initial ambassadors from task completers; give review/training privileges based on demonstrated conduct, not vanity badges. Community risk assessment and moderator capacity.
- Run one public challenge per profession with real outcome rubrics, no simulated standings. One partner employer/educator session.
- Scale newsletter only if readers click through to actionable outcomes and correction rates stay low.
- Trial first paid team/workshop pilot, collect real before/after time/correction data.

### Days 61–90: refine moat and unit economics
- Expand to 10–15 verified labs only if the first 3 prove reuse and retention; keep topic ontology small.
- Test optional consumer Pro pricing with authentic cohorts (not pop-up discounts for unproven value).
- Secure 2–5 paid B2B pilots with written scope/privacy/review and measure delivery margins.
- Implement Portuguese+English SEO route strategy and corrections workflow, track search console with evidence not promises.
- Decide expand/hold/rethink using four-week cohorts, source quality, cost per reviewed output and paid pilot margins.

## 10. Proposed north-star scorecard and decision rights

**North Star:** weekly unique users who actually complete a useful, reviewed AI outcome, with a method/evidence receipt. No passive video plays, pageviews, card clicks, arbitrary streaks or unverified agent claims.

Product: lab-start→reviewed-output %, time to first useful outcome, D7/D28 meaningful return, accessibility/device errors, share→qualified task, user corrections, task value.
Trust: source provenance completeness, stale/unavailable source count, supported claims %, review latency, corrections and source/translation drift.
Community: first project submitted/reviewed, reviewer SLA, constructive replies per project, reports and appeals, retention of legitimate contributors.
Economics: cost per accepted task, inference tokens, creator acquisition spend per activated member, conversion/churn and gross margin, B2B revenue per independently successful pilot.
Safety: unauthorized access attempts, private info logging, consent and deletion, source license, no hidden advertiser influence, human publication approval.

**Stop/pivot rules:** No genuine task completion after 2 weeks of human usability iteration; organic acquisition only generates passive clicks; retention below cohort threshold after usefulness fixes; moderator demand exceeds capacity; user source accuracy issues or privacy leak; gross contribution turns negative; public brand clearance unavailable.

## 11. Executive decision stack

**Do now:** brand clearance, mobile first-win, source-grounded actions, one trustworthy weekly digest, onboarding with no mandatory login, user interviews and small business workshops.
**Do later:** multiple professions, local ambassadors, private project review, paid model usage, team workspaces, YouTube channel expansion.
**Avoid:** rebuilding 200-source news index to compete head-to-head with IA Brasil, 1,000 untested prompts, starting with a paywalled Discord, inflated follower/badge claims, mass AI-generated SEO/news without citation review, automatic unsolicited WhatsApp/newsletter sending, treating model output as independently verified.
